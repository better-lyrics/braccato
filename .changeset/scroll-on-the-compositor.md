Line scrolls no longer stutter with the main thread. The per-line scroll animations ran in additive mode, which Chromium refuses to composite, so every scroll was ticked by the main thread alongside the highlight work; they now replace, and a line still mid-scroll carries its current offset into the next animation instead of stacking a second one. The off-screen line culling observer is rooted on the scroller rather than the window, so its overscan band applies at all (an observer rooted above a scroll container is still clipped by it, and lines were being laid out in the frame they entered view), the band is widened past the browser's own `content-visibility: auto` threshold so toggles no longer land on lines the browser renders anyway, and toggles that fall outside the visible box wait until the scroll animations end. A line sliding to make room for a decoration that streams in mid-song carries that offset into a scroll that starts on it, instead of being replaced by it, and scroll offsets a theme sets in percent are resolved against the line before being carried.
