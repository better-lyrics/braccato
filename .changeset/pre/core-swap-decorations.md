Lyrics swapped into the song on screen no longer carry a translation or romanization onto a line it was not made for. A line whose words changed in the swap, or a different line paired into its place, used to keep what the host had hung on the line before it, so an English line could show the romanization and translation of the Korean line it replaced. As in 1.x, a line keeps what was hung on it only when it is the same lyric, such as one that was only retimed.
